Primal Elemental Rune (1449)

Required Level: 75

Prerequisite: None

Talk To: Rune Master of Resplendency

Message: Well then, you are the one studying the elemental powers, I take it. Those Amplified Runes are rather ... obvious.




Rewards:

  • Yes ... I may just be able to do so. You will however need to gather a number of items. Firstly you will need an elemental crystal for each of the 5 elements, they can be found on the elemental beings here in the Astral World.

Talk To: Rune Master of Resplendency

Message: Did you find the elemental crystals?




Conditions:

  • Collect: 1 Holy Elemental Crystal

  • Collect: 1 Arcane Elemental Crystal

  • Collect: 1 Fire Elemental Crystal

  • Collect: 1 Kinetic Elemental Crystal

  • Collect: 1 Shadow Elemental Crystal

Rewards:

  • You have received 500,000 experience!

  • Very good. Now I will require an item that has been stolen from this sanctuary by the Elemental Betrayer. Bring me back the Ravaged Artifact and I will begin to craft your rune.

Talk To: Rune Master of Resplendency

Message: Did you collect the artifact from the Elemental Betrayer?




Conditions:

  • Collect: 1 Ravaged Artifact

Rewards:

  • You have received 500,000 experience!

  • Well done, we we're nearly there. The last component I require are Elemental Fusers, you can obtain them by speaking with the Elemental Overlord.

Talk To: Rune Master of Resplendency

Message: Did you obtain the Elemental Fusers? <br><br> <b>*Note: Remove any required items from your vault*<b>




Conditions:

  • Collect: 10 Elemental Fuser

  • Collect: 1 Basic Elemental Rune

  • Collect: 1 Amplified Holy Rune

  • Collect: 1 Amplified Arcane Rune

  • Collect: 1 Amplified Fire Rune

  • Collect: 1 Amplified Kinetic Rune

  • Collect: 1 Amplified Shadow Rune

Rewards:

  • You have received 1,000,000 experience!

  • Splendid work champion, here is your Primal Elemental Rune. If you wish to ascend this rune to the highest of levels, speak with me again.

  • You have received 1 Primal Elemental Rune!
